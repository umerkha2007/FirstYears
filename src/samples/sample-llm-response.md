# Portion Sizes for 6-Month-Old Baby

Great question! At **6 months old**, portion sizes should be kept small as your baby is just beginning their solid food journey. Here's what you need to know:

## Starting Portions

- **First foods**: Start with **1-2 teaspoons** of pureed food
- **Gradually increase**: Work up to **1-2 tablespoons** per feeding over a few weeks
- **Frequency**: Offer solids **1-2 times per day** initially

## Key Guidelines

### Listen to Your Baby's Cues
Your baby will show you when they're:
- **Hungry**: Leaning forward, opening mouth, showing excitement
- **Full**: Turning head away, closing mouth, losing interest

### Don't Force It
> ⚠️ **Important**: Never force your baby to finish a portion. Let them decide when they've had enough.

## Sample Meal Plan

| Time | Food Type | Portion |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Morning | Single-grain cereal | 1-2 tablespoons |
| Afternoon | Pureed vegetable | 1-2 tablespoons |

## Safety Considerations

- ✅ **Always supervise** feeding times
- ✅ Ensure food is **smooth and lump-free**
- ✅ Test **temperature** before serving
- ✅ Introduce **one new food at a time** (wait 3-5 days between new foods)

## Additional Tips

1. **Breast milk or formula remains primary nutrition** at this age
2. Solids are for *practice* and *exploration* initially
3. Expect **messy meals** - it's part of learning!
4. Keep a **food diary** to track new introductions and reactions

## When to Consult Your Pediatrician

Please contact your pediatrician if you notice:
- Persistent refusal to eat
- Choking or gagging frequently
- Signs of allergic reaction (rash, vomiting, diarrhea)
- Concerns about growth or development

---

*Remember: Every baby is unique! These are general guidelines, and your pediatrician can provide personalized advice based on your baby's specific needs.*
